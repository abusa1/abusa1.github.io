Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Application Purpose

This application is used exclusively for personal Google Drive access through Rclone.

Information Accessed

The application accesses Google Drive files and folders as authorized by the Google account owner.

How Information Is Used

Google Drive information is used solely for personal file management, including browsing, uploading, downloading, and organizing files.

Data Storage

Rclone stores authentication credentials locally. File data may be cached locally during transfers.

Data Sharing

This personal application does not sell or intentionally share Google Drive information with third parties.

Access Revocation

The account owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account security settings.
